Omni Health

Nutrition & hydration, like a thoughtful dietitian

Log what you eat by typing or snapping a photo. Omni reads your day from Apple Health and tells you the one thing worth doing next, in a single sentence.

  • Cycle-aware nutrition. Suggestions adapt to each phase; data stays in Apple Health.
  • Cook Now. Step-by-step recipes with one-tap timers and Live Activities.
  • Two-way Apple Health sync. Water, meals, workouts and sleep, including deletions.
  • 22 languages. Plus Apple Watch, widgets, Siri and iPad.
