Privacy

This site doesn’t track you.

In short: no cookies, no analytics, no ads. This page covers beyazlabs.com only; our apps have their own privacy policies.

What we don’t collect

We use no cookies. There is no Google Analytics, ad pixel or other tracking tool. Pages load no fonts, scripts or images from other sites.

The one thing kept in your browser

Your language choice (English or Turkish) is stored only in your own browser so it is remembered next time. It is never sent to us.

The contact box

What you type into “Write the email” is not sent to any server; it only creates a draft in your own mail app. Sending it is up to you.

If you email us, we use your name, address and message only to reply and prepare a quote, and we don’t share them with third parties. Our email is routed through Cloudflare and stored in Gmail.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your emails to us deleted, write to enes@beyazlabs.com.
