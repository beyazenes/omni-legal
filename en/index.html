Omni Health

Nutrition and hydration, like a thoughtful dietitian.

Today

One sentence about the one thing that matters.

Omni reads your day from Apple Health (water, meals, workouts and sleep) and tells you what's worth doing next. No dashboards to decode.

Cycle-aware nutrition

Your nutrition, in sync with your cycle.

Suggestions adapt to each phase. Cycle data stays in Apple Health and on your device, and Omni won't guess when it can't read your cycle reliably.

Recipes

Recipes that start from your own kitchen.

Three options shaped by your goals, allergies and the cuisine you grew up with. Log what you ate in one tap.

Cook Now

The timer is already in the step.

If a step says "five minutes", the timer is one tap away. It keeps time on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while you cook.
